the whole idea

No sign-up. No server. No history. Just the people already around you, and a place to talk.

what it does

Made for sharing with people nearby.

No cloud, no middle-man — whatever you send goes straight from your device to theirs.

send anything

Send any file, not just photos

Messages, photos, and any file at all — documents, videos, zip archives — go straight to the person you pick, at full quality.

any device to any device

Mac to phone, phone to PC

Send freely between Mac, Windows, Android and iPhone. A file from your laptop lands on someone's phone just the same.

hands-off

Files arrive on their own

Switch on auto-accept and what people send saves straight to your device — files to Downloads, photos to your gallery. Nothing to tap.

discovery

Everyone just shows up

Open Near and everyone on the same network appears — no codes, no adding contacts.

room + 1:1

The whole room, or just one

Send to everyone at once, or open a private thread with any one person.

no record

Gone when you close it

Near keeps no history. Close the app and the conversation is gone — never saved anywhere, never sent to a server.

nothing is saved
how it works

Three steps, no accounts in sight.

Open the app — it handles the finding, connecting and sending for you.

01 · discover

Everyone shows up

Open Near and everyone else on the same Wi-Fi appears on their own — no codes to share, no contacts to add.

02 · connect

A direct line

The moment you both open it, your devices link up straight to each other — nothing in the middle, nothing to set up.

03 · talk

Send anything, keep nothing

Text, images and files go straight to the other side. Close the app and it's all gone — nothing saved, nothing uploaded.

across networks

Far apart, still direct.

Scan a code once, and two of your devices can reach each other from any network — home Wi-Fi to the office, or a laptop to a phone on 4G.

same network · no server

On the same Wi-Fi, nothing in between

Devices on the same network find each other and connect directly. No server is involved at all — nothing leaves the room.

across networks · paired

Pair once, connect from anywhere

Scan the code on your other device. From then on, a small coordination server only introduces the two — once they're connected, messages and files flow device to device, end-to-end encrypted.

no relay

Direct, or not at all

If the networks in between won't allow a direct connection, Near says so — it never falls back to sending your data through a server. The server only learns which paired devices are online, never what you send.

data never touches a server
